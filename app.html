Backup

The whole system as one file: workers, sites, engineers, attendance, payroll summaries, adjustments, suppliers, the material list, stock movements and material requests. An automatic backup is taken once a day, keeping a rolling 40 days. Backups you take yourself are kept permanently. Anyone can take or download one; only an admin can restore or delete.
