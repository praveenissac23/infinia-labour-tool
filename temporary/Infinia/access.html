Roles

A role is a named set of rights - Assistant Accountant, Purchase Manager, Store Keeper. Give a login a role and it gets exactly those screens; change the role and every login that carries it changes with it.

Settings is on every role - it is where anyone changes their own password. Admin logins always have everything and cannot be given a role.

Logins

Who can sign in, and which role each follows. A login without a role keeps whatever rights it was given in the app's Settings.
