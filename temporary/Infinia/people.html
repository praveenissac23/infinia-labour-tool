New loan

Written to the same loan register the salary cycle reads: the instalment comes off each month's salary from the next cycle, and the balance shows here, on the payroll Loans tab and on the statement remark.

Increment

Written to the payroll Increments register. By default it all goes to the allowance; move part of it to basic only when that was agreed - gratuity is worked out on the basic.

Repayment

Money recovered outside payroll - cash back, or a settlement. Salary instalments are taken by the cycle itself when it is approved.
